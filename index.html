My Story

Hi! I'm Nitisha Dhanavde, a passionate learner with an exceptional love for mathematics and problem-solving. Numbers, logic, and analytical thinking come naturally to me — solving complex problems doesn't feel like work, it feels like play. I'm driven by curiosity and the excitement of finding solutions, and I take pride in pushing my mind to think beyond the obvious.

Outside academics, I'm a dedicated CrossFitter and runner, constantly chasing progress, strength, and personal records. If I'm not studying, you will most definitely find me training — whether it's lifting, running, conditioning, or taking on high-intensity challenges like HYROX. Fitness isn't just a hobby for me, it's a discipline and lifestyle that shapes who I am.

I'm also a huge food enthusiast who loves exploring new cuisines, flavors, and food adventures from around the world — from everyday comfort meals to experimental dishes that most people wouldn't dare to try. Whether it's travel-inspired food like my time in Thailand or something completely new, I'm always ready for a culinary experience.

When I want to slow down and recharge, I turn to drawing, where creativity flows effortlessly and gives balance to my otherwise intense, goal-driven routine.

In short, I'm someone who believes in challenging limits — mentally, physically, and creatively.
